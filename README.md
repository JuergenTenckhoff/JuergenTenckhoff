# Dr. Jürgen Tenckhoff

**Independent Researcher · Engineer · Sociologist**

I work at the intersection of theoretical engineering, systems theory, artificial intelligence, and social theory.

My current research develops the **Theory of Quantum Monads**, a relational framework for studying complex systems, cognition, communication, and artificial intelligence through coherence and interaction.

Building on this work, I have developed the **architecture and formal specification of a Generation III AI system** — a coherence-based approach intended to move beyond conventional language-model optimisation. A major focus for **2027** is the independent implementation of a first working system based on this architecture.

## Open-source work

I use GitHub mainly for reproducible testing, issue reports, technical discussion, and feedback on open-source software.

My practical interests include Linux and self-hosted infrastructure, Nextcloud, AI systems, scientific and technical software, and photographic workflows. I regularly test software with real-world systems, hardware, and data and contribute detailed findings when something can be improved.

GitHub will also increasingly become part of my own development work as the Generation III AI architecture moves from theoretical specification toward implementation.

## Background

My background combines theoretical electrical engineering with sociology and interdisciplinary research.

I previously worked in senior management positions in telecommunications, including Deutsche Telekom and T-Mobile, with responsibilities spanning technology, European projects, standardisation, and strategic development.
